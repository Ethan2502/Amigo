Amigo

This is my project that I got an idea for when my phone's alarm went off. I always tend to use my phone a little bit in bed when I wake up. I thought I needed my phone to wake me up but then I thought of a product that replaced all the "utility" items your phone could do but without the distractions. I also thought to take this a step further and make a product that could block screentime on your phone. 

Why does it need to physically block screentime and not an app? I used apps before but they always make it in fingers reach to disable it. So this product is a phone buddy to help you stay focused!

This Amigo can also be a cool desk toy when you're working by displaying pictures on a "slide deck". 

So in total this Amigo can:
1. Wake you up
2. Block your phone
3. Set timers, stopwatches, and other simple stuff your phone can do.
4. I was also thinking that small kids in elementary school can also try this out as a way to develop more coginitve function like memory games or reaction time things. It can teach them stuff by making a a game where they can answer questions using the keypad as arrows or other controls to move, fight, etc. The benefit of this is the product is a lot cheaper too than buying a brand new computer or phone. So it's a budget friendly calssroom toy!

I used a bunch of stuff on this project for the first time like Ki Cad to make my pcbs. 
In my pcbs I have an ESP with all the stuff connected like speakers, screens, charging, and some other stuff.

I have another 3x3 button matrix pcb connected to my main board with jumper cables. 

For the software I am planning to make some firmware so people can also program, download/make games, and customize it for their needs.
I want it to have a over the wifi updates so it can get better and better software.

Very excited to be in this stage to start building it.
